Why Every Insurance Broker Should Sell Cyber Insurance

The Numbers Don't Lie

The 5-Step Cyber Insurance Sales Process

Step 1: Create Urgency with Risk Assessment

Start every conversation with a free cyber risk assessment. Use tools like:

  • CISA Cyber Essentials - Free government tool
  • Carrier assessment tools (Coalition, At-Bay provide free scans)
  • Simple questionnaire highlighting vulnerabilities

Step 2: Quantify Their Exposure

Make the risk real with specific numbers:

💰 Cost Calculation Script

"Based on your industry and size, a data breach would cost approximately $[X]. Here's the breakdown:"

  • Notification costs: $[number of records × $3]
  • Legal fees: $50,000 minimum
  • Business interruption: $[daily revenue × 7 days]
  • Reputation damage: 23% customer loss

"Your total exposure is roughly $[total]. Cyber insurance costs about 1% of that."

Step 3: Present the Solution

Position cyber insurance as business continuity, not just coverage:

  • Immediate response team: 24/7 breach coaches and IT forensics
  • Financial protection: Covers costs you calculated
  • Reputation management: PR support and credit monitoring
  • Legal compliance: Helps meet contractual requirements

Step 4: Overcome Objections

Use data to counter resistance:

  • "We're too small" → "43% of attacks target small businesses"
  • "We have IT security" → "95% of breaches are due to human error"
  • "It's too expensive" → "It's 80% cheaper than a single incident"

Step 5: Close with Urgency

✅ Closing Script

"I can get you bindable quotes from 3 carriers by tomorrow. The process takes 15 minutes, and coverage can start immediately. Given that ransomware attacks happen every 11 seconds, would you prefer morning or afternoon for our follow-up?"

"Our IT handles security" "Great! Cyber insurance works WITH your IT. When they can't stop an attack, insurance covers the costs." 82% of breaches involve human element

Lead Generation Strategies for Cyber Insurance

1. Mine Your Existing Book

Your current clients are your goldmine. Run this analysis:

Frequently Asked Questions from Brokers

Do I need special licensing to sell cyber insurance?
No special license required - your current P&C license covers cyber. However, brokers with cyber certifications earn 35% more commission. Consider getting certified through Advisen or The Institutes.
How long does it take to quote and bind cyber coverage?
For small businesses (<$5M revenue), you can get bindable quotes in 24 hours. Mid-market takes 3-5 days. Large accounts need 2-3 weeks. Using automated platforms reduces quote time by 75%.
What's the minimum premium to make cyber sales worthwhile?
Target minimum $1,500 annual premium for profitability. At 20% commission, that's $300 upfront plus renewals. Focus on businesses with $1M+ revenue or 10+ employees for best results.
How do I compete with direct cyber insurance companies?
Emphasize your value: claims advocacy, coverage comparison, bundling discounts, and personal service. Broker-sold policies have 43% better claims outcomes than direct purchases.
What's the best way to get appointed with cyber carriers?
Start with Coalition, At-Bay, or Corvus - they have easy online appointments. Show potential volume (your existing book) and complete their training. Most approve brokers within 48 hours.
