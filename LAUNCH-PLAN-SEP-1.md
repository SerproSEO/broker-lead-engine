## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Issues & Solutions
1. **Technical failures**: Have manual backup processes
2. **No initial leads**: Prepared list of 100 warm contacts
3. **Payment issues**: Multiple payment options ready
4. **Website downtime**: CloudFlare CDN and backup hosting
5. **Legal concerns**: Attorney on standby

### Contingency Plans
- If no sales by day 3: Pivot messaging
- If technical issues: Manual outreach
- If low response: Increase ad spend
- If overwhelmed: Prioritize high-value tasks
