## 4. PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE

### 4.1 90-Day ROI Guarantee Terms
Agency guarantees that within ninety (90) days from the Effective Date, Client will achieve "ROI Positive" results, defined as:

**"ROI Positive"** means the total value of qualified leads generated through Agency's services, as measured by Client's documented average customer lifetime value multiplied by the number of leads generated, exceeds the total fees paid to Agency during the guarantee period.

### 4.2 Guarantee Conditions
This guarantee is contingent upon:
- Client maintaining agreed minimum ad spend
- Client providing accurate conversion and revenue data
- Client following Agency's recommendations
- Client having effective sales process (industry-standard close rates)
- No significant market disruptions or force majeure events
- Client not making unauthorized account changes

### 4.3 Refund Process
If ROI Positive status is not achieved:
1. Client must notify Agency in writing by day 100
2. Client must provide documented lead and revenue data
3. Agency will review performance within 10 business days
4. If guarantee conditions were met, Agency will refund 100% of service fees
5. Refund does not include ad spend or third-party costs

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 5.1 Initial Term
Three (3) month minimum commitment starting from Effective Date

### 5.2 Renewal
Month-to-month after Initial Term unless either party provides 30 days written notice

### 5.3 Termination
Either party may terminate:
- For convenience after Initial Term with 30 days notice
- For material breach with 10 days notice to cure
- Immediately for non-payment exceeding 30 days

### 5.4 Effect of Termination
- Client pays all outstanding fees
- Agency delivers final reports and transfers account access
- Confidentiality obligations survive
- Beta benefits (free AI scanner) continue if granted
