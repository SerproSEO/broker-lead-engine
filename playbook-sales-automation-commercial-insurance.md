## Lead Scoring Framework {#lead-scoring}

### Point-Based Scoring Model

**Demographic Scoring (0-40 points)**
```
Company Size:
- 1-5 employees: 5 points
- 5-20 employees: 10 points
- 20-50 employees: 15 points
- 50+ employees: 20 points

Industry Risk Profile:
- Low risk: 5 points
- Medium risk: 10 points
- High risk (healthcare, construction): 20 points

Annual Revenue:
- Under $500K: 5 points
- $500K-$2M: 10 points
- $2M-$10M: 15 points
- $10M+: 20 points
```

**Behavioral Scoring (0-60 points)**
```
Website Engagement:
- Visited pricing page: 10 points
- Downloaded resource: 15 points
- Used calculator/tool: 20 points
- Visited 5+ pages: 10 points

Email Engagement:
- Opened 3+ emails: 5 points
- Clicked link: 10 points
- Replied to email: 20 points

Form Submissions:
- Contact form: 15 points
- Quote request: 25 points
- Demo request: 30 points
```

### Lead Categories

**Hot Leads (80-100 points)**
- Immediate sales outreach
- Personal phone call within 1 hour
- Custom proposal within 24 hours

**Warm Leads (50-79 points)**
- Accelerated nurture sequence
- Sales touch within 48 hours
- Educational content focus

**Cold Leads (20-49 points)**
- Standard nurture sequence
- Monthly check-ins
- Content marketing focus

**Not Qualified (<20 points)**
- Newsletter only
- Quarterly touches
- General education

## Automated Workflows {#workflows}

### Workflow 1: New Lead Onboarding

**Trigger:** Form submission or email signup

**Actions:**
```
Immediate (0-5 minutes):
1. Send welcome email with resource
2. Add to CRM with tags
3. Assign lead score
4. Notify sales if hot lead

Day 1:
5. Send industry-specific guide
6. Connect on LinkedIn (if B2B)

Day 3:
7. Educational email #1
8. SMS check-in (if phone provided)

Day 7:
9. Case study email
10. Calendar link for consultation

Day 14:
11. Break-up email if no engagement
12. Move to long-term nurture
```

### Workflow 2: Quote Request Automation

**Trigger:** Quote request form submission

**Actions:**
```
Immediate:
1. Send confirmation email
2. Create high-priority task for sales
3. Send SMS to sales rep
4. Score as hot lead

Within 5 minutes:
5. Sales rep calls (or automated SMS)
6. Send calendar link if no answer

Within 1 hour:
7. Send preliminary information packet
8. Add to "Quote Requested" pipeline

Within 24 hours:
9. Send personalized video
10. Provide quote or timeline

Days 2-7:
11. Follow-up sequence if no response
12. Provide additional resources
```

### Workflow 3: Re-engagement Campaign

**Trigger:** No engagement for 30 days

**Actions:**
```
Day 30:
1. "We miss you" email
2. Offer valuable new resource

Day 37:
3. Industry news update
4. Case study relevant to them

Day 45:
5. Special offer or incentive
6. Limited time element

Day 60:
7. Break-up email
8. Move to quarterly touches

Day 90:
9. Final valuable resource
10. Unsubscribe if no engagement
```

### Workflow 4: Referral Partner Automation

**Trigger:** Referral received

**Actions:**
```
Immediate:
1. Thank partner via email
2. Notify sales team
3. Fast-track lead scoring

Within 1 hour:
4. Contact referred lead
5. Mention referral source

Within 24 hours:
6. Update partner on progress
7. Send gift card if appropriate

After Close:
8. Notify partner of success
9. Process referral payment
10. Request testimonial
```

## CRM Configuration {#crm}

### Pipeline Stages

**Standard B2B Insurance Pipeline:**
```
1. New Lead (0-24 hours)
   - Auto-assign based on territory
   - Send welcome sequence
   - Score and categorize

2. Qualified (1-7 days)
   - Needs identified
   - Budget confirmed
   - Decision maker engaged

3. Needs Analysis (7-14 days)
   - Current coverage reviewed
   - Gaps identified
   - Savings calculated

4. Quote Provided (14-21 days)
   - Formal quote sent
   - Follow-up scheduled
   - Objections documented

5. Negotiation (21-30 days)
   - Terms adjusted
   - Final proposal sent
   - Close plan created

6. Closed Won/Lost (30+ days)
   - Reason documented
   - Referral requested
   - Upsell identified
```

### Custom Fields Setup

**Lead Information:**
- Current carrier
- Current premium
- Renewal date
- Coverage types needed
- Industry classification
- Employee count
- Annual revenue

**Engagement Tracking:**
- Lead source
- First touch date
- Last activity date
- Email opens/clicks
- Resources downloaded
- Quote requested date
- Objections noted

### Automation Rules

**Rule 1: Hot Lead Alert**
```
IF lead score > 80
THEN: 
- Send SMS to sales
- Create high-priority task
- Send calendar link to lead
```

**Rule 2: Stale Deal Warning**
```
IF no activity > 7 days
THEN:
- Alert deal owner
- Trigger re-engagement email
- Update stage to "At Risk"
```

**Rule 3: Win-Back Campaign**
```
IF deal lost > 90 days ago
AND lost reason = "Timing"
THEN:
- Add to win-back sequence
- Update lead score
- Notify sales
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-automating:** Keep personal touch for high-value interactions
2. **Ignoring data:** Review metrics weekly and adjust
3. **Set and forget:** Automation needs constant optimization
4. **Poor list hygiene:** Clean data regularly
5. **Generic messaging:** Personalize based on industry/size
6. **No testing:** Always A/B test before scaling
7. **Complicated workflows:** Start simple, add complexity gradually
8. **Ignoring compliance:** Maintain CAN-SPAM compliance
9. **No backup plan:** Have manual processes ready
10. **Expecting immediate results:** Allow 60-90 days for optimization
