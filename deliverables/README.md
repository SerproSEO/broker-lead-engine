## Project Overview

This comprehensive SEO strategy and 12-week content plan targets the professional liability insurance marketing niche, with a focus on capturing first-mover advantages in underserved market segments. The strategy is based on analysis of local content ideas and WebSearch research into market trends, competition, and opportunities.

## Methodology

### Research Sources
- **Local Content Analysis**: Analyzed existing content strategy documents from `contentideas/` folder
- **External Research**: WebSearch tool used for SERP analysis, competition research, and market trends (Perplexity MCP not available)
- **Market Analysis**: Focused on professional liability insurance ($44.96B market) and cyber insurance ($16.3B growing 10% annually)

### Keyword Research & Scoring
- **Volume Estimates**: Based on WebSearch findings and content analysis (ranges provided due to tool limitations)
- **Difficulty Scoring**: Relative scale 1-100 based on competition analysis and SERP features
- **Opportunity Scoring**: Formula combining intent fit, SERP weakness, and brand fit

### Opportunity Score Calculation
```
opportunity_score = (intent_fit × 0.4) + (serp_weakness × 0.3) + (brand_fit × 0.3)

Where:
- intent_fit: 0-100 (how well keyword matches business goals)
- serp_weakness: 0-100 (how weak/competitive current SERP is)
- brand_fit: 0-100 (how well keyword fits brand positioning)
```

### Difficulty Score Methodology
```
difficulty_score = base_competition + domain_authority_bonus + serp_features_penalty

Factors:
- Base Competition: 1-70 (keyword competitiveness)
- Domain Authority: +5-15 (strength of ranking domains)
- SERP Features: +5-15 (presence of rich features)
```

## Technical Implementation

### On-Page SEO Standards
- **Title Tags**: Primary keyword + secondary + location (≤60 chars)
- **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling + CTA + keyword inclusion (≤155 chars)
- **URL Structure**: Clean, keyword-rich slugs
- **Header Structure**: H1 with primary keyword, H2s with related terms
- **Internal Links**: 5-10 relevant internal links per page
- **Schema Markup**: Article, Organization, FAQPage, Service schemas

### Content Quality Requirements
- **E-E-A-T Compliance**: Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
- **YMYL Standards**: Your Money or Your Life content guidelines
- **Word Count Targets**:
  - Landing pages: 2,500-4,000 words
  - Blog posts: 2,000-3,000 words
  - How-to guides: 1,500-2,500 words
  - FAQ pages: 500-1,000 words
