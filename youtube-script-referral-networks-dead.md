  - Invite top 20 clients quarterly
  - They refer because they're invested
  - 73% more referrals than asking cold

### Strategy #3: Authority Content Marketing
• **Become the expert** others refer to:

• **You refer first** strategy:
  - Send clients to trusted vendors
  - Track every referral you make

• **The psychology:** People feel obligated to return favors
• **Success rate:** 67% reciprocation within 90 days
