## 🎯 CONTENT OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY

### **On-Page SEO Standards**:
1. **Title Tags**: Primary keyword + secondary + location (where applicable)
2. **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling + CTA + keyword inclusion
3. **Headers**: H1 with primary keyword, H2s with related terms
4. **Content**: 5-10% keyword density, natural language
5. **Internal Links**: 5-10 relevant internal links per page
6. **External Links**: 2-3 authoritative external sources

### **Technical SEO Requirements**:
1. **Page Speed**: <3 seconds load time on mobile
2. **Mobile Optimization**: Responsive design, mobile-first
3. **Schema Markup**: Service, Organization, FAQ schemas
4. **Image Optimization**: Alt tags, compressed files
5. **URL Structure**: Clean, keyword-rich URLs
6. **SSL Certificate**: HTTPS throughout site

### **Content Quality Standards**:
1. **Expertise**: Industry-specific knowledge and insights
2. **Experience**: Real-world examples and case studies
3. **Authoritativeness**: Cited sources and expert quotes
4. **Trustworthiness**: Transparent, accurate information
5. **User Value**: Actionable advice and practical guidance
