7 Mistakes Insurance Agencies Make With Google Ads (And How to Fix Them)

By David Park • Sept 3, 2024 • 5 min read

After auditing over 200 Google Ads accounts for insurance agencies, I've seen the same mistakes repeated again and again. These aren't minor optimizations—they're fundamental errors that waste thousands of dollars every month.

The good news? They're all fixable. And once you fix them, your cost per lead will drop by 40-60% almost immediately.

The Problem With Insurance Google Ads

Insurance keywords are expensive. We're talking $40-$80 per click for competitive terms. At those prices, you can't afford to make mistakes. Yet most agencies are lighting money on fire with poorly configured campaigns.

Let's fix that.

1

Bidding on Broad Match Keywords

This is the cardinal sin of Google Ads. Broad match keywords will match to anything Google thinks is "related." I've seen "cyber insurance" trigger ads for "cyber Monday deals."

The Fix: Use phrase match or exact match only. Yes, your volume will decrease, but your relevance will skyrocket.

2

Ignoring Negative Keywords

Without negative keywords, you're paying for searches like "free insurance," "insurance jobs," and "insurance memes" (yes, really).

The Fix: Add at least 200 negative keywords before launching. Update weekly based on search term reports.

3

Sending Traffic to Your Homepage

Your homepage is designed to serve everyone. Your ads should send people to pages designed to convert that specific search.

The Fix: Create dedicated landing pages for each ad group. Match the headline to the ad copy exactly.

"We reduced our cost per lead by 67% just by creating service-specific landing pages. It was the easiest win we've ever had."
— Jennifer Ross, Digital Marketing Director
4

Not Using Ad Extensions

Ad extensions are free real estate. They increase your ad size by 2-3x and improve click-through rates by 30%.

The Fix: Use all relevant extensions: sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call extensions, and location extensions.

5

Competing on Price

Insurance isn't a commodity. Stop advertising "cheapest rates" and start advertising value, expertise, and trust.

The Fix: Focus on your unique value proposition. What makes you different? Lead with that.

6

Ignoring Mobile

62% of insurance searches happen on mobile. Yet most agencies have desktop-only strategies.

The Fix: Create mobile-specific ads with click-to-call buttons. Ensure your landing pages load in under 3 seconds.

7

Set and Forget Mentality

Google Ads isn't a Ronco Rotisserie. You can't set it and forget it. The algorithm changes, competitors adjust, and performance drifts.

The Fix: Review campaigns weekly. Test new ads monthly. Audit thoroughly quarterly.

The Path Forward

Fixing these seven mistakes won't make you a Google Ads expert overnight. But it will stop the bleeding and give you a fighting chance against larger competitors with bigger budgets.

Remember: In Google Ads, perfection is impossible, but profitability is achievable. Focus on incremental improvements, and compound gains will follow.

The agencies that win at Google Ads aren't the ones with the biggest budgets—they're the ones that make the fewest mistakes.
