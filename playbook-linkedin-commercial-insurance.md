## Targeting Strategy {#targeting}

### Search Filters for Prospects

**Primary Filters:**
- Title: CEO, CFO, President, Owner, Risk Manager
- Company Size: 11-200 employees
- Industries: Professional Services, Healthcare, Technology, Manufacturing
- Geography: Your service area

**Boolean Search Strings:**
```
(CEO OR CFO OR "Chief Financial Officer" OR President OR Owner) 
AND ("professional services" OR healthcare OR technology OR manufacturing)
NOT insurance NOT broker
```

### Target Lists by Insurance Type

**Cyber Insurance Prospects:**
- Technology companies
- Healthcare providers
- Financial services
- E-commerce businesses
- Any company handling customer data

**Professional Liability Prospects:**
- Consulting firms
- Law firms
- Accounting firms
- Architecture/Engineering firms
- Marketing agencies

**General Commercial Prospects:**
- Manufacturing
- Construction
- Retail
- Wholesale/Distribution
- Real estate

## Message Sequences {#messages}

### After Connection Acceptance

**Message 1: Thank You + Value** (Day 1)
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting! I noticed [Company] has been [specific observation about 
their business]. 

I put together a quick guide on the top 5 commercial insurance mistakes 
[industry] companies make. Would you like me to send it over? No sales pitch, 
just helpful information.

Best,
[Your name]
```

**Message 2: Industry Insight** (Day 7)
```
Hi [Name],

Thought you might find this interesting - [Industry] businesses that added 
cyber insurance last year paid 40% less in premiums than those buying it now 
(source: Industry report 2024).

The market is hardening quickly. Happy to share what I'm seeing if helpful.

[Your name]
```

**Message 3: Soft CTA** (Day 14)
```
Hi [Name],

Quick question - how often does [Company] review its commercial insurance 
portfolio? 

Most [industry] businesses find savings or coverage gaps with an annual review. 
If you're open to it, I could provide a no-obligation coverage analysis.

Worth a brief call?

[Your name]
```

**Message 4: Break Up** (Day 21)
```
Hi [Name],

I've shared a few resources about commercial insurance optimization for 
[industry] companies. Since I haven't heard back, I'll assume this isn't 
a priority right now.

If anything changes, I'm here. Wishing [Company] continued success!

[Your name]
```

## Compliance & Limits {#compliance}

### LinkedIn's Weekly Limits

**Connection Requests:**
- Week 1-3: 20-50 per week
- Week 4+: Up to 100 per week
- Never exceed 100/week

**Messages:**
- 50-75 per day maximum
- Space throughout the day
- Avoid copy-paste identical messages

**Profile Views:**
- 100-150 per day maximum
- Use private mode if researching competitors

### Warning Signs to Stop
- "You're moving too fast" warnings
- Connection request pending review
- Messaging temporarily restricted
- Account verification requests

### Insurance Compliance on LinkedIn

**Avoid:**
- Specific quotes or rates
- Guarantees of coverage
- Misleading credentials
- Unauthorized carrier representations

**Include:**
- General education
- Industry trends
- Risk management tips
- Compliance updates
