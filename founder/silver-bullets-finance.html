Profit Is Not Optional

Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is king. These strategies ensure you keep more than you make and build real wealth, not just big numbers.

1

Revenue is Vanity, Profit is Sanity, Cash is King

I'd rather make $10K with 90% margins than $100K with 9% margins. Focus on keeping money, not just making it.

Profit-First Framework:

  • Calculate desired profit first, then price accordingly
  • Cut any expense that doesn't directly generate revenue
  • Track profit margin weekly, not just monthly
  • Choose high-margin services over high-revenue ones
  • Say no to low-margin work, even if it's easy money
2

The 10x Rule for Tools

Only buy tools that return 10x their cost. This filter alone will save you from 90% of bad investments.

Tool Investment Criteria:

  • Will it save 10x its cost in time?
  • Will it generate 10x its cost in revenue?
  • Will it prevent 10x its cost in mistakes?
  • If no to all three, don't buy it
  • Track actual ROI after 90 days
3

Recurring Revenue or Death

One-time sales are a job. Recurring revenue is a business. Transform everything into a subscription.

Recurring Revenue Transformation:

  • Add maintenance to every one-time service
  • Create membership tiers for ongoing value
  • Bundle services into monthly packages
  • Offer "insurance" or "protection" plans
  • Build in automatic renewals and upgrades
4

The 80% Automation Target

Every business process should be 80% automated. The remaining 20% is where humans add value.

Automation Economics:

  • Calculate cost per manual task
  • Automate anything under $50/hour value
  • Reinvest savings into more automation
  • Track time saved and convert to dollar value
  • Compound automation returns monthly
5

Price for Profit, Not Market Rate

Market rate is what broke businesses charge. Price based on value delivered and profit needed.

Profit-Based Pricing:

  • Calculate all costs including your time
  • Add desired profit margin (minimum 50%)
  • That's your floor price, period
  • Add value to justify premium pricing
  • Never compete on price, compete on value
6

The Cash Buffer Rule

Always have 6 months of expenses in cash. This buffer lets you make strategic decisions, not desperate ones.

Buffer Building Strategy:

  • Calculate true monthly burn rate
  • Save 20% of revenue until buffer is built
  • Never touch it for "opportunities"
  • Use it to say no to bad clients
  • Let it earn interest while sitting
7

Expense Audits Save Fortunes

Every month, kill one expense. Most businesses are bleeding money they don't even notice.

Monthly Expense Audit:

  • List all recurring expenses
  • Calculate ROI for each
  • Cancel anything below 3x return
  • Negotiate everything else down
  • Replace expensive tools with cheaper alternatives
8

Collection is Sales Part 2

A sale isn't complete until the money is in your bank. Chase payments like you chase sales.

Collection System:

  • Invoice immediately upon delivery
  • Follow up at 3, 7, 14, 21 days
  • Automate payment reminders
  • Offer payment plans before they ask
  • Cut off service for non-payment
9

The Pareto Principle Portfolio

20% of clients generate 80% of profit. Fire the bottom 20% every quarter.

Client Portfolio Optimization:

  • Rank clients by profit contribution
  • Identify bottom 20% dragging you down
  • Raise prices or fire them
  • Replace with higher-margin clients
  • Repeat quarterly for continuous improvement
10

Compound Everything

Every dollar saved or earned should generate more dollars. Money sitting still is money dying.

Compounding Strategy:

  • Reinvest 30% of profit into growth
  • Automate investing of surplus cash
  • Buy assets that generate passive income
  • Build systems that scale without you
  • Let time and math build wealth
📈

Base Trading: The Quick Fingers Luc Strategy

Buy the panic, sell the relief. When crypto crashes through established support levels (bases), that's when smart money accumulates.

The QFL Base Trading Method:

  • Identify Strong Bases: Look for horizontal support that held multiple times
  • Wait for the Crack: Price must break below base with volume
  • Buy the Panic: Set orders 5-15% below the broken base
  • Sell the Bounce: Take profits when price returns to base
  • Risk Management: Never risk more than 2% per trade

Why Base Trading Works:

  • Markets overreact to fear - panic selling creates opportunities
  • Strong bases act like magnets - price tends to return
  • Clear entry and exit points - no guessing
  • Works in all market conditions - bear markets are best
  • Repeatable and systematic - not based on predictions

The Setup Process:

  • Scan for coins with clear horizontal bases (3+ touches)
  • Mark bases on higher timeframes (4H, Daily)
  • Set alerts for when price approaches base
  • Place limit orders below base when crack occurs
  • Set take-profit orders at base level (or slightly below)

Key Rules for Success:

  • Never chase - wait for price to come to your orders
  • Stronger the base, better the trade
  • More panic = more profit potential
  • Take profits quickly - this is trading, not investing
  • Keep position sizes small - preserve capital for more setups
