The Unlikely Journey

From a 10-year-old immigrant with big dreams to a tech entrepreneur who believes AI will save us all (spoiler: it will)

The Beginning

I came to America when I was 10 years old. While other kids were mastering Fortnite, I was mastering English and figuring out how this whole "American Dream" thing worked. Turns out, it involves a lot of hustle, coffee, and believing in yourself when nobody else does.

As a DACA recipient, I learned early that nothing is guaranteed except what you build yourself. So I decided to build... everything.

"Fun fact: I graduated business school with straight A's. This has been absolutely crucial to my success, right up there with knowing how to tie my shoes and remembering to breathe. My clients are constantly asking to see my GPA before signing contracts."

— Said no successful entrepreneur ever

The Real Education

While my perfect GPA was collecting dust, I was getting my real education in the trenches of digital marketing. Over the past decade, I've learned that success isn't about grades—it's about solving real problems for real people.

I discovered my superpower: making businesses visible online and turning that visibility into cold, hard revenue. Turns out, helping businesses make money is a pretty good way to make money. Who knew?

The AI Revelation

For over a decade, I've believed that AI will lift humanity into unprecedented prosperity. Not in some sci-fi dystopian way, but in a "let's automate the boring stuff so humans can be more human" way. Every line of code I write, every system I build, is a step toward that future.

What Drives Me

At 28, I work from home, focused on three things: building wealth, creating systems that work without me, and preparing for the AI revolution that's already here.

My mission is simple: Use technology and marketing to help small businesses thrive. Build generational wealth. Then use that wealth to lift others up. Rinse and repeat until we've all made it.
