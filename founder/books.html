My Reading Philosophy

📚 Read for ROI, Not Entertainment

Every book should pay for itself 100x over. If I can't extract at least one actionable strategy that makes money, it was a waste of time.

🎯 Implementation Over Information

I'd rather deeply implement one book than superficially read 100. Knowledge without action is worthless.

🔄 Re-read the Classics

I re-read The E-Myth every year. Each time I'm at a different level, I extract different lessons. Great books grow with you.

⚡ Speed Reading is Overrated

Read slowly, think deeply, implement immediately. One fully absorbed chapter beats 10 skimmed books.
