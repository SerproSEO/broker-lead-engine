The AI Revolution Is Here

For over a decade, I've believed AI will lift humanity into unprecedented prosperity. These are the strategies I use every day to make that vision a reality—one business at a time.

1

AI is a Leverage Multiplier, Not a Replacement

Use AI to 10x your output, not to replace thinking. The best results come from AI-assisted humans, not AI alone.

How to Apply This:

  • Use AI for first drafts, then add your expertise for the final 20%
  • Let AI handle data analysis while you focus on strategic decisions
  • Combine AI's speed with your industry knowledge for unbeatable results
  • Think of AI as your intern with perfect memory and infinite patience
2

Train Your AI Like an Employee

Feed it your best work, your tone, your strategies. The more context you give, the better it performs.

Training Framework:

  • Create a "knowledge base" document with your best examples
  • Include your writing style, common phrases, and industry jargon
  • Provide examples of what good vs bad output looks like
  • Update your training data weekly based on what works
3

Automate the Boring, Innovate the Important

Let AI handle data entry, initial drafts, and research. You handle strategy, relationships, and creative breakthroughs.

Task Division Strategy:

  • AI does: Research, data processing, first drafts, scheduling
  • You do: Final decisions, relationship building, creative strategy
  • Together: Content creation, analysis, problem-solving
  • Never AI alone: Client relationships, financial decisions, brand voice
4

The API Economy is Your Friend

String together APIs like Legos. OpenAI + Zapier + Airtable = a business that runs itself.

Essential API Stack:

  • OpenAI/Claude for content and analysis
  • Zapier/Make for workflow automation
  • Airtable/Notion for data management
  • Stripe/PayPal for payment automation
  • SendGrid/Mailgun for email automation
5

Prompt Engineering is the New Programming

Master the art of talking to AI. The right prompt can mean the difference between garbage and gold.

Prompt Framework:

  • Role: "You are a [specific expert]..."
  • Context: "Given [specific situation]..."
  • Task: "Create/analyze/write [specific output]..."
  • Format: "Structure it as [specific format]..."
  • Constraints: "Make sure to [specific requirements]..."
6

AI Memory Systems Beat Human Memory

Build AI systems that remember everything about your clients, projects, and processes. Perfect recall = perfect service.

Memory Architecture:

  • Client profiles with every interaction logged
  • Project histories with decisions and outcomes
  • Process documentation that updates itself
  • Conversation threads that maintain context forever
7

The 80/20 Rule of AI Implementation

80% of the value comes from 20% of AI features. Focus on high-impact, easy wins first.

High-Impact AI Wins:

  • Email drafting and responses (saves 5 hours/week)
  • Content creation and repurposing (saves 10 hours/week)
  • Data analysis and reporting (saves 8 hours/week)
  • Customer service automation (saves 15 hours/week)
8

AI-First Process Design

Don't retrofit AI into old processes. Design new processes with AI capabilities in mind from the start.

Process Redesign Method:

  • Map current process and identify AI opportunities
  • Redesign with AI doing heavy lifting
  • Add human checkpoints for quality and decisions
  • Build in continuous learning and improvement
9

The Compound Effect of AI Systems

Each AI system you build makes the next one easier and more powerful. They stack and multiply.

System Stacking Strategy:

  • Start with data collection AI (foundation)
  • Add analysis AI (insights)
  • Layer on content creation AI (output)
  • Connect with automation AI (scale)
  • Top with optimization AI (improvement)
10

AI Ethics = Business Advantage

Be transparent about AI use. Clients appreciate honesty and the value you add on top of AI.

Ethical AI Framework:

  • Always disclose AI assistance when asked
  • Position AI as enhancing, not replacing, your service
  • Maintain human oversight on all critical decisions
  • Use AI to deliver more value, not cut corners
  • Share AI benefits with clients through better pricing
