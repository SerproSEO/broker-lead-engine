- Primary: Deep Blue (#1E3A8A) - Trust & stability
- Accent: Bright Green (#10B981) - Growth & success  
- Alert: Orange (#F59E0B) - Urgency & CTAs
- Background: Off-white (#FAFAFA) with white sections
- Text: Dark gray (#111827) with lighter gray (#6B7280) for secondary

## Interactive Elements

### Hover Effects
- Service cards: Subtle lift with shadow
- CTAs: Color shift to darker shade
- Stats: Gentle pulse animation

### Micro-Interactions
- Numbers count up when scrolled into view
- Progress bars animate for stats
- Smooth scroll between sections
- Floating chat widget (bottom right)

### Trust Signals Throughout
- 🔒 SSL badge in footer
- 📊 Live "leads generated today" counter
- ⭐ Rating badges (if available)
- 🏆 "90-Day Guarantee" badge floating

### Mobile Optimizations
- Sticky header with hamburger menu
- Touch-friendly CTAs (min 44px)
- Stacked sections
- Swipeable testimonials
- Click-to-call buttons

## Conversion Optimization

### Primary CTA: "Get Free Market Analysis"
- Appears 4 times (hero, middle, bottom, sticky)
- Orange color for maximum contrast
- Leads to assessment form

### Secondary CTA: "Apply for Beta"
- Creates urgency with limited spots
- Qualification requirements visible
- Premium positioning

### Exit Intent Popup
"Wait! See how many leads you're missing..."
- Triggered at 70% scroll or exit intent
- Simple email capture
- Offers market report download

This design balances professionalism with urgency, using the beta positioning to create scarcity while building trust through the guarantee and real results.
