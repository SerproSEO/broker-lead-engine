## 1. 🔍 SERP API - Rankings & Competition Analysis

### What It Does:
- Retrieves top 100 search results from multiple search engines
- Provides real-time SERP data with location/language targeting
- Supports Google, Bing, YouTube, Yahoo, Baidu, Naver, Seznam

### Key Endpoints for Insurance Marketing:
- **Organic Results**: Track keyword rankings
- **Local Pack**: Monitor local insurance service visibility
- **SERP Features**: Track featured snippets, knowledge panels
- **Screenshots**: Visual SERP analysis

### Practical Applications for Your Business:

#### 🎯 Rank Tracking Strategy
```
Track these keywords daily:
- "professional liability insurance [city]"
- "cyber insurance marketing"
- "insurance agency SEO"
- "E&O insurance [location]"
```

#### 🔥 Competitor Intelligence
- **Monitor competitor rankings** for your target keywords
- **Analyze their SERP features** (reviews, maps, snippets)
- **Track their local visibility** in different markets
- **Identify ranking opportunities** they're missing

#### 📍 Local SEO Monitoring
- Track Google My Business rankings
- Monitor local pack appearances
- Analyze location-based search results
- Identify local keyword opportunities

### Cost Structure:
- **Live Method**: Instant results ($0.0015-$0.006 per task)
- **Standard Method**: Scheduled retrieval (cheaper)
- **Sandbox**: Free testing environment
