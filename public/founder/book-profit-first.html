Transform Your Business from a Cash-Eating Monster to a Money-Making Machine

Why This Book Changed Everything

This book flipped my entire financial mindset. Instead of hoping for profit at year-end, I take it first. Result? My businesses are now profit-generating machines, not cash-burning hopes.

Key Concepts That Matter

1. The Profit First Formula

Traditional: Sales - Expenses = Profit. Profit First: Sales - Profit = Expenses. Take your profit first, then run your business on what's left.

How I Apply This:

Every dollar that comes in: 15% to profit, 30% to taxes, 50% to operations, 5% to owner's pay. Profit is no longer optional.

2. Multiple Bank Accounts System

Create separate accounts for Profit, Tax, Operating Expenses, and Owner's Pay. Allocate percentages to each on the 10th and 25th.

How I Apply This:

I have 5 business bank accounts. Money flows automatically into each. Can't spend tax money on operations—it's not there.

3. Parkinson's Law Applied to Money

Expenses expand to consume available resources. By limiting available money for expenses, you force innovation and efficiency.

How I Apply This:

Cut operating account by 20%. Forced to eliminate waste and negotiate better deals. Business runs leaner and meaner.

4. The Instant Assessment

Calculate your real revenue (total sales minus materials/subcontractors). This shows your true business size and appropriate profit targets.

How I Apply This:

Realized my $500K business was really a $200K business after materials. Adjusted targets and strategies accordingly.

My Top 10 Takeaways

  • Take profit first, always—even if it's just 1%
  • Multiple bank accounts create automatic discipline
  • Small plates = smaller portions (use smaller bank accounts)
  • Your business should serve you financially from day one
  • Cut expenses ruthlessly—you won't miss what you eliminate
  • Revenue size doesn't matter if there's no profit
  • Quarterly profit distributions keep you motivated
  • Remove temptation by hiding money from yourself
  • Gradual implementation works—start with 1% profit allocation
  • Profit is not an event, it's a habit
