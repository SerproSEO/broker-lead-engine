Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical engagement look like?

I work with businesses in three ways: Monthly retainers for ongoing marketing/automation, project-based work for specific implementations, or strategic consulting for high-level planning. Most clients start with a project and move to retainer once they see results.

How quickly can you start?

For the right project, immediately. I keep my client roster small to maintain quality, so if we're a good fit, I can usually begin within a week. Rush projects are possible with adjusted pricing.

What industries do you work with?

While I have deep expertise in insurance, e-commerce, and local services, I work with any industry that's ready to embrace automation and modern marketing. If you sell to businesses or consumers and want to grow, we can work together.

Do you guarantee results?

Yes, with conditions. For SEO and lead generation, I offer performance guarantees after an initial assessment. If I don't deliver the agreed results, you get your money back. I only take on clients where I'm confident I can deliver.

What makes you different from other agencies?

I don't just talk about AI and automation—I live it. My entire business runs on systems I've built. When I help you automate, it's based on real experience, not theory. Plus, I work with a small number of clients to ensure exceptional results.
