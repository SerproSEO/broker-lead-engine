Marketing Is Math, Not Magic

Every marketing dollar should return $3 minimum. If it doesn't, you're doing charity, not business. These strategies turn marketing from expense to investment.

1

The Money is in the Unsexy Niches

Everyone targets "entrepreneurs." I target "commercial insurance brokers in Massachusetts." Guess who makes more money?

Niche Selection Formula:

  • High ticket value ($5,000+ average sale)
  • Clear pain points you can solve
  • Reachable through specific channels
  • Low competition from generalists
  • Repeat purchase potential
2

SEO is a Real Estate Game

Every keyword is digital real estate. Buy low (long-tail keywords), develop the property (content), rent it out forever (organic traffic).

SEO Real Estate Strategy:

  • Find keywords with 100-1000 searches/month
  • Create the best content on the internet for that keyword
  • Build supporting content around it
  • Internal link everything strategically
  • Wait 6 months, collect rent forever
3

Cold Email is Not Dead, Your Approach Is

Personalization at scale wins. I send 1,000 emails that feel like 1-to-1 conversations.

Cold Email That Works:

  • Research the prospect for 2 minutes minimum
  • Reference something specific about their business
  • Lead with value, not your pitch
  • One clear call-to-action
  • Follow up 7 times minimum
4

Your Best Marketing is Your Worst Client's Complaint

Fix what people hate about your industry. That's the entire marketing message.

Complaint-to-Campaign Process:

  • List top 5 industry complaints
  • Pick the one you can genuinely solve
  • Make that your unique selling proposition
  • Build everything around that promise
  • Guarantee it to remove risk
5

Content is a Compound Asset

Every piece of content should work for you forever. Build once, benefit indefinitely.

Compound Content Strategy:

  • Create evergreen content that stays relevant
  • Update old content instead of creating new
  • Repurpose across 5+ channels
  • Build topic clusters for authority
  • Internal link to spread authority
6

The Rule of 7 Still Rules

People need to see your message 7 times before buying. Most marketers quit at 2.

7-Touch System:

  • Touch 1-2: Awareness (ads, content)
  • Touch 3-4: Interest (email, retargeting)
  • Touch 5-6: Consideration (case studies, demos)
  • Touch 7+: Decision (offers, guarantees)
  • Automate all 7 touches across channels
7

Local + Digital = Monopoly

Dominate locally online and you'll never compete on price again.

Local Domination Playbook:

  • Claim and optimize all directory listings
  • Get 100+ consistent citations
  • Generate reviews systematically
  • Create location-specific content
  • Run geo-targeted ads with local offers
8

Attribution is Everything

If you can't track it, you can't scale it. Know exactly where every dollar comes from.

Attribution Framework:

  • UTM parameters on everything
  • Call tracking numbers for each campaign
  • Conversion tracking on all platforms
  • CRM integration for full journey
  • Monthly ROI report by channel
9

Speed Beats Perfection

Launch at 70% perfect and improve. Perfectionists go broke waiting for 100%.

Rapid Launch Method:

  • Set a 48-hour launch deadline
  • Get minimum viable campaign live
  • Test with $100 budget
  • Optimize based on real data
  • Scale what works, kill what doesn't
10

The Best Marketing is Not Marketing

Build something so good that customers market it for you. Word of mouth beats any ad.

Creating Marketing Machines:

  • Overdeliver on every promise
  • Build referral incentives into the product
  • Make sharing easy and rewarding
  • Create "wow" moments worth talking about
  • Turn customers into evangelists
