# Programmatic SEO Strategy - Broker Lead Engine
*Created: 2025-09-01*

## Objective
Build a programmatic SEO system that automatically generates thousands of high-quality, unique pages targeting our 1000 keywords through templates and data combinations.

## Potential Scale
With our keyword research, we can create:
- 150 location pages (cities/states)
- 120 industry pages
- 150 x 120 = 18,000 location + industry combinations
- 100 problem/solution pages
- 150 x 100 = 15,000 location + problem combinations
- **Total potential: 33,000+ unique pages**

## Page Templates Needed

### 1. Location Template
Pattern: /[state]/[city]/cyber-insurance-marketing/
Variables: {city}, {state}, {population}, {businesses}, {competitors}

### 2. Industry Template  
Pattern: /industries/[industry]/cyber-insurance-leads/
Variables: {industry}, {risks}, {regulations}, {average_premium}

### 3. Location + Industry Template
Pattern: /[state]/[city]/[industry]-cyber-insurance/
Variables: All combined

### 4. Service + Location Template
Pattern: /[service]/[location]/
Variables: {service}, {location}, {pricing}, {results}

### 5. Comparison Template
Pattern: /compare/[competitor1]-vs-[competitor2]/
Variables: {features}, {pricing}, {reviews}

## Data Sources Needed
- City/state databases with business counts
- Industry risk profiles
- Competitor information
- Local search volumes
- Regulatory requirements by state

## Technical Implementation
- Dynamic page generation
- Unique content spinning
- Schema markup automation
- Internal linking matrix
- Sitemap generation

## MVP Approach
Start with highest ROI combinations:
1. Top 50 cities + cyber insurance
2. Top 10 industries + top 50 cities
3. Top 10 problems + top 50 cities
Total: 1,050 pages in Phase 1

## Expected Results
- 50,000-100,000 organic visitors/month within 6 months
- 500-1,000 leads/month from organic
- Dominate local and industry searches
