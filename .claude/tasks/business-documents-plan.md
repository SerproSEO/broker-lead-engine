# Business Documents Creation Plan
*Created: 2025-09-01*

## Objective
Create essential business documents for Broker Lead Engine to operate legally, onboard clients, and deliver services professionally.

## MVP Approach
Focus on documents needed to:
1. Onboard first 3 beta clients legally
2. Deliver services with clear expectations
3. Track finances and performance
4. Maintain basic compliance

## Priority 1: Day-One Essentials (Need Immediately)

### Legal Protection
1. **Digital Marketing Services Agreement**
   - Covers Facebook Ads, Google Ads, SEO services
   - 90-day ROI guarantee terms (carefully worded)
   - Payment terms, termination clauses
   - IP rights and data usage

2. **SaaS License Agreement** 
   - AI Lead Scanner licensing terms
   - Per-user license structure
   - Data privacy and security
   - Usage limitations

3. **Non-Disclosure Agreement**
   - Protect client data and strategies
   - Mutual confidentiality
   - Standard 2-3 year term

### Client Onboarding
4. **Client Intake Questionnaire**
   - Business information
   - Current marketing efforts
   - Goals and KPIs
   - Access credentials needed
   - Brand guidelines

5. **Welcome Packet**
   - What to expect
   - Timeline and milestones
   - Communication protocols
   - Your team and roles

### Financial
6. **Invoice Template**
   - Professional branded design
   - Payment terms (Net 15)
   - Late payment fees
   - Payment methods accepted

7. **Simple Financial Tracker**
   - Revenue by client
   - Expenses by category
   - Monthly P&L
   - Cash flow tracking

## Priority 2: Week 2-3 Documents

### Operational
8. **Service SOPs**
   - Facebook Ads setup process
   - Google Ads campaign creation
   - SEO audit checklist
   - Monthly reporting process

9. **Proposal Template**
   - Service packages
   - Pricing breakdown
   - Timeline
   - Expected results

### Compliance
10. **Insurance Marketing Compliance Checklist**
    - Prohibited terms
    - Required disclosures
    - State-specific requirements

## Legal Considerations

### Must Include in Service Agreement:
- Clear definition of "ROI positive"
- Specific conditions for guarantee
- Client responsibilities
- Limitation of liability
- Dispute resolution (arbitration)

### Insurance Compliance Notes:
- Cannot guarantee specific insurance sales
- Must avoid prohibited marketing terms
- Need disclaimers about results
- State-specific requirements vary
