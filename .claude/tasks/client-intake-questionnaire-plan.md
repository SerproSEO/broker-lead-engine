# Client Intake Questionnaire - Plan
*Created: 2025-09-01*

## Objective
Create a comprehensive intake form to gather all necessary information from cyber insurance agencies before starting services.

## Key Information to Gather
1. Business basics (name, structure, contacts)
2. Current marketing efforts and results
3. Target market and ideal clients
4. Competition and positioning
5. Access credentials needed
6. Goals and success metrics
7. Budget and resources
8. Compliance and licensing status

## Format Requirements
- Professional PDF format
- Fillable form fields
- Clear sections and instructions
- Space for detailed responses
- Checkbox options where appropriate

## Sections to Include
1. Company Information
2. Current Marketing Status
3. Target Market Definition
4. Goals & KPIs
5. Competition Analysis
6. Budget & Resources
7. Access & Credentials
8. Compliance & Legal
9. Communication Preferences
10. Additional Notes
