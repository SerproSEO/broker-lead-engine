# Cyber Insurance Market Research Plan
*Created: 2025-09-01*

## Objective
Deep dive into cyber insurance and professional liability insurance market to understand:
- Pain points of agencies selling these products
- Customer acquisition challenges
- Marketing strategies that work
- Website architecture that converts

## Research Areas

### 1. Market & Industry Analysis
- Current market size and growth projections
- Key players and competition
- Regulatory environment
- Emerging trends

### 2. Agency Pain Points
- Lead generation challenges
- Sales process friction
- Competition from direct carriers
- Technology adoption barriers
- Compliance complexities

### 3. Buyer Behavior
- How businesses search for cyber insurance
- Decision-making process
- Key concerns and objections
- Trust factors

### 4. Marketing Strategies
- What's working in 2024-2025
- Digital vs traditional approaches
- Content that converts
- SEO opportunities

### 5. Website Best Practices
- Conversion elements
- Trust signals
- Content structure
- Lead capture optimization

## MVP Approach
Focus on actionable insights that directly inform:
1. Website structure and content
2. Marketing messaging
3. Service positioning
4. Lead generation strategy

## Expected Deliverable
Comprehensive research document that becomes the foundation for all marketing efforts
