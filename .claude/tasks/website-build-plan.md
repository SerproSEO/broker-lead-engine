# Website Build Plan - Broker Lead Engine
*Created: 2025-09-01*

## Objective
Build a complete website for Broker Lead Engine that converts cyber insurance agencies into clients, based on competitor research and market analysis.

## Research Phase
1. Analyze successful cyber insurance marketing websites
2. Identify conversion elements that work
3. Document messaging and positioning strategies
4. Map out complete site architecture

## Competitors to Research
- Coalition (cyber insurance leader)
- Corvus Insurance (tech-forward approach)
- At-Bay (prevention-focused)
- CyberPolicy (broker marketplace)
- Embroker (digital broker)
- Insurance agency marketing companies

## Key Elements to Analyze
- How they position their value proposition
- Lead capture mechanisms
- Trust signals and social proof
- Content structure and navigation
- Conversion tools (calculators, assessments)
- Call-to-action strategies

## Expected Deliverable
Complete multi-page website with:
- Homepage
- Service pages
- Industry pages
- Risk assessment tool
- Resource center
- Pricing page
- About/Contact pages

All optimized for converting $3M+ cyber insurance agencies into beta clients.
