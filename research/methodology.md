# SEO Research Methodology - Professional Liability Insurance Marketing

**Research Date**: 2025-09-21
**Locale**: US-en
**Scope**: Professional liability insurance marketing and related sectors
**Data Sources**: Manual research + WebSearch validation (DataForSEO MCP unavailable)

## Scoring Framework

### Intent Mapping (0-100 scale)
**Commercial Intent (75-100)**:
- Keywords with buying signals: "best", "compare", "cost", "quote", "services"
- Industry-specific professional services: "legal", "medical", "technology"
- Geographic + service combinations: "New York professional liability"

**Informational Intent (25-75)**:
- Educational queries: "what is", "how to", "guide", "tips"
- Industry awareness: "trends", "requirements", "compliance"
- Problem-solving: "why", "when", "should I"

**Navigational Intent (10-25)**:
- Brand-specific searches
- Direct navigation queries

### Keyword Difficulty (KD) Estimation
**Formula**: `KD = base_competition + domain_authority_penalty + serp_features_penalty`

**Base Competition (1-70)**:
- Professional liability insurance: 15-25 (low - first mover)
- Cyber insurance: 20-30 (moderate - growing market)
- Insurance agent SEO: 40-60 (high - established market)
- General insurance terms: 70+ (very high - dominated by giants)

**Domain Authority Penalty (+5-15)**:
- Major insurance companies in SERP: +15
- Established SEO agencies: +10
- Insurance trade publications: +5

**SERP Features Penalty (+5-15)**:
- Featured snippets present: +5
- Local pack dominance: +10
- Shopping/ads heavy: +15

### SERP Weakness Analysis (0-100 scale)
**High Weakness (75-100)**:
- Thin content (< 1000 words)
- Mismatched intent (product pages for informational queries)
- Poor E-E-A-T signals
- Outdated content (> 2 years old)
- Generic content (non-industry specific)

**Moderate Weakness (40-75)**:
- Some content gaps
- Mixed intent signals
- Moderate authority sites

**Low Weakness (0-40)**:
- Strong, comprehensive content
- Perfect intent match
- High authority domains

### Brand Fit Assessment (0-100 scale)
**High Fit (75-100)**:
- Professional liability focus (perfect alignment)
- Insurance agency target market
- Geographic relevance (NY/CT/NJ)
- Service-aligned keywords

**Moderate Fit (40-75)**:
- Related insurance sectors
- Broader marketing topics
- Adjacent professional services

**Low Fit (0-40)**:
- Generic business terms
- Unrelated industries
- Consumer-focused (vs. B2B agency focus)

### Opportunity Score Calculation
```
opportunity_score = (intent_fit × 0.4) + (serp_weakness × 0.3) + (brand_fit × 0.3)

Rationale:
- Intent fit (40%): Most important for conversion alignment
- SERP weakness (30%): Ranking feasibility
- Brand fit (30%): Strategic alignment with business goals
```

## E-E-A-T Framework

### Expertise Signals
- Industry-specific terminology usage
- Technical accuracy in insurance concepts
- Regulatory compliance awareness

### Experience Signals
- Case studies and client examples
- Practical implementation guidance
- Results-oriented content

### Authoritativeness Signals
- Industry expert citations
- Official source references
- Professional credentials mention

### Trustworthiness Signals
- Transparent methodologies
- Contact information and credentials
- Privacy policy and disclaimers
- Regular content updates

## Rate Limiting & Quality Control

### Manual Research Limits
- Maximum 50 keywords per research session
- 30-minute intervals between intensive research
- Quality over quantity approach

### Validation Process
1. Cross-reference with industry reports
2. Validate against competitor analysis
3. Confirm with target audience insights
4. Test assumptions with pilot content

## Reproducibility Steps

1. **Seed Extraction**: Parse contentideas/ directory for topics
2. **Keyword Expansion**: Use semantic analysis and industry knowledge
3. **Intent Classification**: Apply scoring framework consistently
4. **Cluster Assignment**: Group by topic and intent similarity
5. **Opportunity Scoring**: Apply weighted formula uniformly
6. **Quality Review**: Validate against business objectives
