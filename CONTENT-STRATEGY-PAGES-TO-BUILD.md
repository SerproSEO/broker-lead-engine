## 📈 TIER 4: PROGRAMMATIC SEO PAGES (Build Months 4-6)
*Scalable templates, 1000s of pages potential*

### Template 1: [City] + [Insurance Type]
- Formula: "{City} {Insurance Type} Insurance"
- Example: "Austin Cyber Insurance"
- Scale: 50 cities × 10 insurance types = 500 pages

### Template 2: [Industry] + [Insurance Type] + [State]
- Formula: "{Industry} {Insurance Type} in {State}"
- Example: "Restaurant Liability Insurance in California"
- Scale: 20 industries × 5 insurance types × 50 states = 5,000 pages

### Template 3: [Insurance Type] + Cost + [City]
- Formula: "How Much Does {Insurance Type} Cost in {City}"
- Example: "How Much Does Cyber Insurance Cost in Seattle"
- Scale: 10 insurance types × 100 cities = 1,000 pages
